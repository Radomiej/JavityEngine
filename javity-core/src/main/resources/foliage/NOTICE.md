# Original foliage atlas

`leaves-and-grass.png` and its source generator `tools/generate-foliage-atlas.py`
were authored for JavityEngine on 2026-10-20. No downloaded textures, photographs,
fonts, models or other source assets are used. Version 1, 1024 × 1024 RGBA.

To the extent possible under law, the authors waive all copyright and related
or neighboring rights to this atlas and its generated geometric demo tree under
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ .
The generator remains available under the repository's source-code license.

Layout: two columns, grass left and leafy twigs right; four variants top to bottom.
Each silhouette has clear margins and RGB padding outside its alpha coverage.
The bundled descriptor additionally insets each cell by two actual texture pixels.
